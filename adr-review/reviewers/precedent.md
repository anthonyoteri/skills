# Precedent reviewer

Review the ADR against the repo's other ADRs.

- **Conflicts**: it contradicts an active ADR without superseding it explicitly. Blocking.
- **Duplicates**: an active ADR already records the same decision, or most of it.
- **Supersession**: an ADR it replaces is named, and that ADR's status and superseded-by link are updated with it. For a merged ADR, a later ADR that replaces it is reflected in its status.
- **Format**: number, title, status, date and sections follow the repo's ADR template or its existing ADRs.
